CT Unit 2: Analysis and Reasoning

Topics

  • Draw reasonable conclusions from statements or data
  • Form hypotheses or conjectures
  • Consider relevant information only
  • Reduce consequences flowing from given data

Examples

  • Analyze an article and identify its main claims and supporting evidence
  • Separate relevant and irrelevant information from a paragraph
  • Draw conclusions from a set of statements: e.g., "All birds can fly; penguins are birds; therefore, penguins can fly" → identify flaw
