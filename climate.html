Climate Change and Sustainability

This module focuses on climate change governance, sustainability practices, international agreements, and policy implications. Students will explore environmental laws, carbon credit mechanisms, sustainability goals, and real-world case studies of climate initiatives.

Unit 1: Climate Governance and International Affairs

  • Global climate agreements (UNFCCC, Paris Agreement, Kyoto Protocol)
  • International organizations and their roles
  • Case studies on international climate disputes
  • National policies in the context of global frameworks
Example:
Analyze how the Paris Agreement encourages countries to reduce emissions and adapt national policies to global climate goals.
Exercise:
  • Choose a country and summarize its commitments under the Paris Agreement.
  • Discuss how international climate policies influence national decision-making.

Unit 2: Climate Theories and Sustainability Practices

  • Theories: carbon cycle, greenhouse effect, global warming
  • Sustainability concepts and frameworks
  • Carbon credits and market mechanisms
  • Role of governments, NGOs, and industries in sustainable development
Example:
Evaluate a case study where carbon trading reduced industrial emissions in a developing country while supporting renewable energy projects.
Exercise:
  • Draw a flowchart showing the carbon cycle and how human activity alters it.
  • Identify 3 local sustainability practices and evaluate their effectiveness.

Unit 3: Policies, Acts, and Sustainability Goals

  • Climate Change Act and national environmental laws
  • Interstate disputes on water, land, and other resources
  • Sustainability goals: SDGs and national targets
  • Government plans, initiatives, and evaluation metrics
Example:
Discuss how India’s National Action Plan on Climate Change aligns with Sustainable Development Goals and addresses energy, water, and forest management.
Exercise:
  • Choose one SDG related to climate and describe how your region contributes to achieving it.
  • Analyze a recent government policy related to sustainability and critique its effectiveness.

Unit 4: Case Studies and Applied Projects

  • Real-world climate projects and interventions
  • Evaluation of sustainability practices in industries and communities
  • Innovations in renewable energy, water management, and urban planning
  • Practical implementation of sustainability projects
Example:
Study a city that implemented green infrastructure to reduce urban heat and improve water management. Analyze outcomes and lessons learned.
Exercise:
  • Design a small sustainability project for your campus or community and outline expected impacts.
  • Research a renewable energy initiative and report on its results and challenges.
