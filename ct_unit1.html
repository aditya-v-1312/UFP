CT Unit 1: Assessment and Evaluation

Purpose

Identify intended and actual inferential relationships in statements, concepts, and representations.

Sub-Skills and Examples

  • Clarifies the purpose: Summarize the main intent of a short article or news piece.
  • Checks accuracy of information: Verify a fact from two reliable sources.
  • Questions assumptions: Identify hidden assumptions, e.g., "All hardworking students get high grades."
  • Uses logic: "If it rains, the ground gets wet. It is raining. Therefore, the ground is wet."
  • Generates opinion: Write a short paragraph stating your viewpoint with reasoning.
