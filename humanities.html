Humanities & Arts

This course provides an interdisciplinary exploration of human culture, society, psychology, economics, and art. Through discussion, critical analysis, and observation, students will gain an understanding of how individual, social, and cultural factors shape human experience.

Main Topics

  • Culture and Society

    Understand culture and society from anthropological and sociological perspectives, explore social stratification, gender roles, governance, and family structures.

  • Introduction to Social Psychology

    Learn how social influences, group behavior, self-concept, attitudes, and cognition shape human interactions and decision-making.

  • Elementary Economics

    Explore foundational concepts of economics, including wealth, welfare, scarcity, economic growth, human wants, utility, and the law of demand.

  • Art Appreciation

    Discover the principles of visual language, different forms of art, how art conveys meaning, and methods for analyzing and appreciating artworks.
