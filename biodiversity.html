Biodiversity and Conservation

This module introduces students to biodiversity, ecological processes, conservation laws, and sustainable practices. Students will learn how to protect ecosystems, understand governance policies, and explore current research trends.

Unit 1: Introduction to Ecology and Biodiversity

  • Definition and importance of biodiversity
  • Levels of biodiversity: genetic, species, ecosystem
  • Ecological interactions: food chains, webs, and trophic levels
  • Threats to biodiversity: habitat loss, pollution, climate change
Example:
Consider a forest ecosystem. Identify producers, consumers, and decomposers. Explain how deforestation can affect this food web and reduce biodiversity.
Exercise:
  • List 5 species in your local environment and classify them as producers, consumers, or decomposers.
  • Identify 3 major threats to biodiversity in your city or region.

Unit 2: Conservation Laws and Governance

  • Biodiversity Act, Wildlife Protection Act, River Protection Laws
  • Roles of government and NGOs in conservation
  • Protected areas: national parks, wildlife sanctuaries, biosphere reserves
  • Case studies: successful conservation projects
Example:
The Gir National Park project successfully protected the Asiatic lion. Analyze the measures taken and how local communities were involved.
Exercise:
  • Choose a local protected area and list the species it conserves.
  • Research one conservation project in India or worldwide and summarize its strategies and outcomes.

Unit 3: Best Practices and Sustainability

  • Habitat restoration and reforestation projects
  • Community-led conservation initiatives
  • Integrating traditional knowledge into modern conservation
  • Current research trends and technological innovations in biodiversity monitoring
Example:
Community-led wetland restoration can improve water quality and support migratory birds. Discuss how local participation increases success.
Exercise:
  • Identify one local ecosystem and propose a small-scale project to enhance its biodiversity.
  • Investigate a recent research paper on biodiversity conservation and summarize key findings.
