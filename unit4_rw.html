Unit 4: Composing a Written Text

1. Introduction / Opening

  • Example: “Rainy days bring joy and reflection, making even ordinary streets magical.”

2. Body / Development

  • Example: “I ran through puddles, laughing as the rain soaked me. Birds chirped in rhythm with the raindrops on leaves.”
  • Use examples, sensory details, and personal reflections to enrich your narrative.

3. Conclusion / Closing

  • Example: “As I returned home, soaked yet happy, I realized that simple moments often leave the most lasting memories.”
  • Summarize key feelings or lessons learned.

4. Supporting Details & Formatting

  • Use descriptive words, dialogues, and transitions between paragraphs.
  • Example: “I said, ‘Watch this!’ and jumped into the puddle. My friends laughed and joined me immediately.”

Practice Exercises

  • Write a short story on the theme “An Unexpected Adventure”.
  • Include at least one dialogue, sensory detail, and reflection.
  • Edit and improve your draft using techniques from Unit 3.
