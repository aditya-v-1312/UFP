Unit 2: Structuring and Developing Write-Ups

1. Note Making and Note Taking

  • Example: Extract key points from a lecture: “Causes of pollution → industrial waste, vehicular emissions, deforestation.”
  • Example: Take brief notes from an article: “Benefits of exercise → improves mood, strengthens heart, boosts immunity.”

2. Plagiarism, Paraphrasing, Acknowledgment

  • Example: Paraphrase “Exercise improves heart health” → “Regular workouts strengthen cardiovascular health.”
  • Always cite sources: “According to WHO (2023)…”.

3. Developing a Structure for the Write-Up

  • Example: Essay structure → Introduction, Body Paragraphs, Conclusion.
  • Example: Story structure → Setting, Characters, Conflict, Resolution.

4. Product/Concept/Process Description & Developing User Manual

  • Example: Describe a smartphone → Features, functions, usage instructions.
  • User Manual Example: Step 1: Charge the device. Step 2: Press power button to switch on.
