Unit 2: Column Analysis & Observe and Write

1. Column Analysis

  • Identify Author’s Viewpoint: Example: Column argues for reducing plastic use. Author believes it is harming oceans.
  • Summarize Key Arguments: Example: Plastic bags pollute water, harm wildlife, and alternatives should be used.
  • Evaluate Evidence: Example: Author cites a report by WWF about marine animals affected by plastic.
  • Notice Style and Tone: Example: Author uses persuasive language, rhetorical questions, and anecdotes to influence readers.

2. Observe and Write

  • Observe a Scene: Example: “The park was alive with children playing, birds chirping, and sunlight filtering through the leaves.”
  • Use Sensory Language: Example: “The aroma of freshly baked bread drifted from the bakery nearby.”
  • Organize Paragraph: Example: First describe setting, then actions, finally reflections.
  • Different Styles: Example: Narrative – “I ran across the bridge…”, Descriptive – “The river shimmered under the moonlight…”, Reflective – “Watching the sunset made me realize the passage of time.”

Practice Exercises

  • Analyze a newspaper column about environmental issues and summarize its main arguments.
  • Write a paragraph describing your classroom using all five senses.
  • Create a reflective paragraph about a recent walk in your neighborhood.
