Unit 1: Introduction to Data Science

What is Data Science?

  • Data Science is the field of study that combines domain expertise, programming skills, and knowledge of mathematics and statistics to extract meaningful insights from data.
  • It applies techniques like data mining, machine learning, predictive analytics, and data visualization to solve real-world problems.

Why is Data Science Important?

  • It helps companies make informed decisions through predictive modeling and trend analysis.
  • Plays a vital role in automation, business optimization, fraud detection, and recommendation systems.

Data Science Workflow

  1. Problem Definition: Understanding the business goal.
  2. Data Collection: Gathering data from different sources.
  3. Data Cleaning: Removing nulls, duplicates, and outliers.
  4. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Visualizing and summarizing datasets.
  5. Model Building: Using machine learning algorithms to train models.
  6. Interpretation & Reporting: Making conclusions and communicating findings.

Skills Required for Data Scientists

  • Programming Languages: Python, R
  • Data Manipulation: Pandas, NumPy
  • Statistics and Probability
  • Data Visualization: Matplotlib, Seaborn
  • Machine Learning: scikit-learn, TensorFlow
  • Communication and Storytelling

Key Roles in Data Science

  • Data Scientist: Designs and builds models to solve business problems.
  • Data Analyst: Interprets data to find trends and patterns.
  • Data Engineer: Builds and manages data pipelines and architecture.
  • Machine Learning Engineer: Builds scalable ML systems for production.

Real-Life Applications

  • Healthcare: Predicting diseases, personalized treatment.
  • Banking: Fraud detection, credit scoring.
  • Retail: Recommendation engines, inventory forecasting.
  • Marketing: Customer segmentation and targeting.
