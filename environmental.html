Environmental Science

This course explores the ecological framework, sustainability, climate change, biodiversity, natural resource management, and waste management. Students will understand environmental processes, governance, policies, and current research trends through case studies, fieldwork, and practical exercises.

Main Modules

  • Biodiversity and Conservation

    Learn the basics of ecology and biodiversity, study conservation laws like the Biodiversity Act, and explore best practices and recent innovations in conservation.

  • Natural Resource Management

    Understand biogeochemical cycles, resource systems, traditional and modern resource conservation methods, and Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA).

  • Climate Change and Sustainability

    Explore international governance, sustainability policies, carbon credit mechanisms, and real-life case studies for sustainable development and climate action.

  • Waste Management

    Study theories, strategies, best practices, legal frameworks, engineering innovations, and practical case studies for effective waste management.
