Unit 1: Understanding and Analyzing Write-Ups

1. Purpose of Reading & Writing

  • Example: Identify whether an article is meant to inform, persuade, or entertain.
  • Example: Determine if a news report aims to educate or a story aims to amuse.

2. Identification of Structure & Analysis

  • Example: Recognize introduction, body, conclusion in an essay.
  • Example: Identify headings, subheadings, and bullet points in an article.

3. Identification of Themes / Arguments

  • Example: Theme of climate change in a news article.
  • Example: Argument for healthy eating in an opinion piece.

4. Rewrite Event / News as a Story

  • Example: News headline “Local School Wins Science Competition” → Story: “Excited students of ABC School celebrated their victory in the interschool science competition, showcasing creativity and teamwork.”
